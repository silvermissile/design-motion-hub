# NOTICE

`motion-recipes-kit` bundles 84 dependency-free motion/shader implementations extracted from [nexu-io/motion-anything](https://github.com/nexu-io/motion-anything) (Apache-2.0).

- **react-bits** (<https://reactbits.dev>): 35+ effects in `assets/effects/` are dependency-free JS/GLSL ports of react-bits components (originally redistributed in `motion-anything` with the author's permission). Keep attribution when redistributing.
- **shaderbg.js**: Upgraded from `motion-anything/recipes/web/_fx/shaderbg.js` with optional `manual` / `attachTimeline` deterministic clock support for OpenMotion HyperFrames.
